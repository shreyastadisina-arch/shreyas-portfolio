About me

Someone who needs to know how things work.

I'm a second-year Computer Science student who gets genuinely excited about technology — not just using it, but opening it up in my head to understand what's happening underneath. When something works, I want to know why. When something is broken or annoying, I start thinking about how it could be better.

I like going past the surface-level explanation of a concept. That habit pulls me toward AI, software, emerging technologies and the machines and systems behind them. I enjoy building websites and experimenting with software, and I also have a creative side: photography and visual design keep me looking at the world differently.

I notice everyday problems and think about practical ways to solve them — which is why I'm drawn to entrepreneurship, startups and understanding markets. I'd love to meet creative people and collaborate on ideas that matter. And I'm deliberately not boxing myself into one specialization yet: I'd rather explore widely, then go deep where I can create the most impact.

My philosophy

“Stay curious. Understand how things work. Build things that matter.”
